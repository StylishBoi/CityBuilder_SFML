---City Builder---

WARNINGS - You might have an error message when you try to launch the executable file, ignore it, it will still open regardless.

CONTROLS - You only need your mouse to play this, everything uses left click to be interacted with

GAMEPLAY - Try to have the biggest city possible on the island you were given.
If NPCs are hungry and can't eat, they will do nothing until they're able to eat.
NPCs will die and their house will be destroyed if they do not have any food for too long.

NPCs - Lumberjack (Gets wood), Miner (Gets stone), Gatherer (Gets food)

ECONOMY - Wood and stone are needed to build buildings, food is needed to keep your NPC alive

SIDE NOTE - I made the procedural generation for the island myself.
